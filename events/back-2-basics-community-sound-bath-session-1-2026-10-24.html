About this session

An elegant, effective community sound bath at an accessible cost — a spacious venue, a small team guiding the experience, and room to simply rest.

This is the 4:00–5:00 pm session (doors 3:40). A second session runs 5:30–6:30 pm.

Please bring your own mat, pillow and blankets — the thicker and cozier the better. A preparation email with all the details follows your registration.

What to expect

Arrival and settling in

You set up your mat, get comfortable, and we do a brief grounding check-in. I'll explain the instruments and what to expect. No pressure, no performance.

The journey

I layer sounds, building and releasing in waves. Your only job is to breathe and let go. Thoughts will come. That's normal. Let them drift past like clouds.

The close

I bring the volume and intensity down gradually, ending in near-silence.

Integration time

A few minutes of quiet silence before anyone moves. Then we come back slowly together.

What to bring

Comfort is the priority. It's the key to dropping in.

  • A yoga mat or thick sleeping pad
  • A blanket — your body temperature drops when you relax deeply
  • A pillow or bolster for under your knees or head
  • An eye mask if you have one
  • Water for after
  • An open mind — that's genuinely all you need

First time? Read What to Expect at Your First Sound Bath in Edmonton. Sound baths are for rest and relaxation, not a substitute for medical care.
