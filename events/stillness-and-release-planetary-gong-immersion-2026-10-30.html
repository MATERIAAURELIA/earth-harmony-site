About this session

6 Large planetary gongs, deep vibrations and resonance felt in an intimate room — thick mats, sheepskins, blankets, bolsters and tea all provided.

The gongs carry you into the deep; your only task is to arrive and let your nervous system find its own way home. A sound journey like no other — a space to be held, for those who hold a lot.

If this session books out, join the waitlist — if there's enough interest I'll open a second sitting the same evening, 8:15–10:00pm. It's a Friday, so a later finish is on the table.

Often called a gong bath — you lie down, the gongs do the rest.

What to expect

Arrival and settling in

You set up your mat, get comfortable, and we do a brief grounding check-in. I'll explain the instruments and what to expect. No pressure, no performance.

The journey

I layer sounds, building and releasing in waves. Your only job is to breathe and let go. Thoughts will come. That's normal. Let them drift past like clouds.

The close

I bring the volume and intensity down gradually, ending in near-silence.

Integration time

A few minutes of quiet silence before anyone moves. Then we come back slowly together.

What to bring

Provided at the home studio: thick mats, sheepskins, blankets, bolsters and tea.

  • An eye mask if you have one
  • Water for after
  • An open mind — that's genuinely all you need
